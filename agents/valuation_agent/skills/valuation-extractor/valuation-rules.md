# Valuation Rules & Business Logic

## Credit Scoring Thresholds
- **Low Risk**: Credit score $\ge$ 700. Eligible for automated approval paths.
- **Medium Risk**: Credit score between 600 and 699. Requires secondary review.
- **High Risk**: Credit score $<$ 600. Requires manual underwriting sign-off.

## Solvency Requirements
- **Debt-Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)**: Must maintain a minimum DSCR of 1.25 for standard commercial and retail loan applications.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**: Secured loans must not exceed an LTV ratio of 80% unless explicit executive exceptions are logged.
